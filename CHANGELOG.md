# Changelog

## 1.37.0 (2026-11-23): 👥 party bars under the minimap, 32 new Labirin monsters
- **Your party under the minimap** (feedback #22): each member's ❤️ HP and 🔷 mana bars and their carried
  Syiling, with 👑 for the leader, ⚔️ while fighting and where they are (here, B3, in town). Tap it for the
  party window.
- **32 new monsters** (feedback #27): every floor now has 8 to 22 kinds (it was 3 to 6), such as Katak Lumpur,
  Kelawar Merah, Golem Ais, Api Biru, Langsuir, Toyol Emas, Mumia Firaun and Naga Jed Kecil. Some are tanky,
  some are fragile but hit hard, some are fast, some are brutes. They're as tough as the monsters they live
  alongside.

## 1.36.0 (2026-11-23): ⚔️ Labirin damage tiers, real gear in fights, furniture variants
- **Skills and ultimates hit much harder than a plain attack now** (feedback #26):
  - ⚔️ Attack ×1;
  - 💥 Skill ×1.2–1.9 (it costs more mana);
  - 📜 Ultimate ×1.5–2.3 on every monster, and spell books ×2.4–3.
  
  The ultimate needs a longer focus bar (6 rounds), and floor bosses have about 30% more HP to match.
- **Everyone fights with the gear they really wear** (feedback #23): no weapon means bare, taped fists 👊,
  and no shield means none. Weapons and shields show their tier's colour on every party member, not just you.
  Party cards show each fighter's ⚔️ ATK, 🛡️ DEF and 💨 SPD.
- **Furniture variants show up** (feedback #25): Shiny, Golden and Rainbow furniture have their own look (a
  sheen, gold, rainbow colours, glints) in the decorate list, in your room, as item icons and for visitors.

## 1.35.0 (2026-11-23): 💥 bigger skills, full-screen ultimates, two idol Teman
- **Labirin HP and mana bars sit right above the hotbar**, exactly as wide as it, on every screen size.
- **💥 Skills hit much harder on screen:** a bigger charge-up, big sparks, more zoom and shake, bigger
  numbers, and each element's own column, bolt or tornado on the monster.
- **📜 Ultimates take over the screen** for a split second: a burst in the element's colour with rays, a
  giant icon and the move's name (fire rises from below, lightning strobes, water ripples, wind gusts,
  stone shakes). Then a bigger hit lands on every monster.
- **Two new idol Teman** in the Telur Teman egg (legendary), drawn like the game's own characters:
  🎤 **Kuki Emas** (black mullet, leather jacket) and 🎀 **Wonnie Bertuah** (long hair, pink stage outfit).
  They wave, sparkle and dance-hop after you.

## 1.34.0 (2026-11-23): 🔥 Labirin elements, mana and a portal warp
- **Free basic skills by element** (feedback #20): 🔥 Api, 💧 Air, ⚡ Petir, 🌪️ Angin and 🪨 Tanah. Equip one
  in 🎒 Bag → 🔥 Elements, or mid-fight with the small ⇄ next to the Skill button (Q on a keyboard). Switching
  doesn't use your turn. Each has its own pros and cons:
  - 🔥 strong hit that keeps burning, but costs the most mana;
  - 💧 heals you part of the damage, but hits softer;
  - ⚡ always strikes first with extra crit, but picks its own target;
  - 🌪️ hits every monster, but is weak against a lone one;
  - 🪨 turns you to stone for the round (less damage), but hits last.
- **🔷 Mana:** skills cost mana now (focus is only for the ultimate). You get some back every round, slowly
  outside fights, faster by the hall's campfire, and resting fills it. New potions: 🥥 Air Kelapa (+50%)
  and 🍯 Madu Kelulut (full). New skill: 🔷 Tenaga Dalam (+10% max mana per rank).
- **Your 📜 Ultimate follows your element:** every element has a free basic one with its own look. A spell
  book of that element makes it stronger (🔥 Kitab Api Pelita for Api, and so on).
- **HP and mana bars** with the numbers inside, on the fight cards and the Labirin HUD.
- **Portal warp:** going into the Labirin (and out through the hall's portal) zooms through a purple swirl.

## 1.16.1 (2026-11-22): green squares on the map, memory, scrolling lists
- **Green squares where the map should be (report #13):** when the background map painter failed on a piece of
  the map, the game kept asking it again every frame, so that piece never appeared. It's now painted the
  normal way instead. If the background painter keeps failing, the game stops using it.
- **Memory (PC hanging at 100% memory):** that endless retry also made fresh full-size pictures every frame,
  so memory filled up faster than the browser could free it. It's gone. The map also no longer keeps a second
  copy of every map picture once it's on the graphics card. That's up to ~7 MB less per map square, and about
  40 squares are loaded on a big screen.
- **Lists jumping back to the top while you scroll (report #12):** the ⚒️ Forge's item list and the Merge all /
  Scrap all window were redrawn every second or so (each money tick) and lost their scroll. They keep it now,
  also when you pick an item.

- **Torque:** strongest in first gear and fading gear by gear. The low gears fly by and the high gears take
  longer. 0-to-top: cars 12.6–15.3 s, bikes 10.3–14.3 s.
- **Settings → 🏁 Rev limiter bounce:** turn it off for a flat high note at the top of each gear.
- **8 directions:** cars and bikes show a 45° view when heading diagonally, for other players' rides too.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)

The first complete version: every phase of the spec (`docs/GAME_SPEC.md` §20) is built and the §19 content list
is met. The full round-by-round log is in `docs/PROGRESS.md`.

### New in the final round
- **🏅 Trophies:** 74 achievements in 9 groups, each paying RM, XP, shards or a potion the moment it's done.
  They stay through a rebirth.
- **📅 Weekly quests:** 3 bigger quests a week, new every Monday, next to the 3 daily ones.
- **🚗 Driving:**
  - a real gearbox: each gear has its own top speed, pull follows a torque curve and the gear, plus air drag;
  - automatic or manual (R up, F down, G toggles), with over-revving downshifts refused;
  - revs that climb smoothly, coasting when you let go;
  - a big dashboard (rev counter, shift lights, km/h, gear) and tyre smoke on hard launches.
- **🔊 Engine sound:**
  - per-cylinder pressure pulses and a ringing pipe;
  - an inline-4 howl, shift cuts with a clunk, and the rev-limiter chop;
  - nearby riders heard from their real speed, panned left/right, as far as their exhaust carries.
- **🚘 Cars:**
  - bigger, and drawn ¾ from above in every direction;
  - about 8% faster at the top, with less torque;
  - at least 2 s in every gear (0-to-top 13.5–19.5 s).
- **🏍️ Bikes:**
  - about 8% faster at the top, with less torque;
  - at least 2 s in every gear (0-to-top 14.8–16.5 s).
- **⚔️ Gua Gelap:**
  - HP carries over between floors;
  - rest rooms (heal, buff, or sacrifice HP for money);
  - Auto keeps going deeper until the whole team faints;
  - an angled 3D cave view and animated ultimates.
- **🐾 Teman gear:** "Wearing: …" on each Teman's page, a 🎽 mark on the list, and a "Put it on a Teman" picker
  from the bag.
- **Sorting:** the bag, Teman, chefs & cashiers, pet gear and the pawn shop all list the rarest first (then tier,
  then variant).
- **🏙️ Ambience:**
  - traffic, birds and crickets in town;
  - a crowd and slot jingles in the casino;
  - a sizzling pan while you cook.
  Settings → Ambient sounds.
- **Fixes:** after a rebirth, open quests counted from the old stat totals.
- The server prints its version on startup.
